## 2026-09-19 — Audit: `@expo/ui`, `expo-glass-effect`, `expo-symbols`

**Question:** these three packages are in `package.json` but are not in the
brief's §2 stack list. Where did they come from, and do they conflict with
building our own tokens and primitives from the board?

**Finding — they are not design-handoff leftovers.** They arrived in commit
`370488e`, whose message is "Initial commit — Generated by create-expo-app
4.0.0". They are part of the default Expo SDK 57 template, which predates the
design export entirely.

**They are also not removable.** All three are direct runtime dependencies of
`expo-router` itself:

```
expo-router@57.0.19 → dependencies: @expo/ui ^57.0.16,
                                    expo-glass-effect ^57.0.1,
                                    expo-symbols ^57.0.2
```

Deleting them from `package.json` would not remove them from `node_modules`;
npm would reinstall them under `expo-router`. The template lists them at the top
level so their native code is autolinked at the correct version. Removing the
top-level entries risks a version mismatch in a native build, for no benefit.

**Decision: keep them installed, import none of them.** Verified that nothing in
`src/` imports any of the three today.

**Do they conflict with our own tokens and primitives? No — provided we never
import them.** The risk is not that they exist, it is what they would do if
used:

- `@expo/ui` renders real SwiftUI controls. They take Apple's system styling and
  cannot be restyled to the board's orange, hand-drawn look — in particular the
  hard-offset bottom-edge button shadow (§3.2) is not expressible. Using one
  would mean shipping a control that looks nothing like the board.
- `expo-glass-effect` is iOS system "liquid glass" material. The board has no
  glass surfaces.
- `expo-symbols` renders SF Symbols — Apple's icon set. Whether it is right for
  our icons depends on what the board draws; that is question 4 below, not a
  decision to make unilaterally.

**Consequence for Phase 0c:** none. `src/theme/tokens.ts` and
`src/components/ui/` get built from the board with plain React Native
primitives, exactly as §4 specifies. No change of approach.

**Rule adopted:** these three are transitive infrastructure for expo-router
only. Anything importing them directly needs a decision recorded here first.
